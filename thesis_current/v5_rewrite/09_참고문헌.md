## Editorial Conventions

1. **IEEE-style ordering.** Within a category, multi-author publications by the same lead author are ordered by year (descending).
2. **Body-to-list mapping.** Every in-text citation in the form "(Author, Year)" or "Author et al. (Year)" is mapped 1:1 to a numbered entry in this list.
3. **Software references.** A small number of essential software tools required to reproduce this dissertation (FAISS, NetworkX) are included in §17 as scholarly citations. Other tools and online resources used during this research are listed in the supplementary section below.
4. **CORRIGENDUM as evidence.** The evaluation-infrastructure rectification reported in Ch. VI §1.4 and §3.3 of this dissertation is published openly via the GitHub repository linked in entry [1].
5. **Total: 17 categories, 100 entries.**
